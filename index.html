03 The Riders

Four players who met on the local circuit and never quite got off it. Jazz Circuit Riders play funk-forward jazz built for movement — horn lines with grease on them, a rhythm section that leaves room, and a set that works as well at a wedding reception as it does at last call.

We bring our own PA, we start on time, and we've never played the same set twice.
